AutoX Live

Support

AutoX Live is an independent iOS app for following autocross timing results, comparing runs with friends, and posting times to community leaderboards. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any event organizer, club, or timing provider.

Get help or send feedback

Email romangarms@gmail.com. Bug reports are most useful with the app version (shown at the bottom of the Setup tab) and the event you were looking at.

Report a leaderboard or a time

Open the leaderboard or the run in the app, tap the menu, and choose Report Leaderboard or Report Run. Reports are reviewed and content that breaks the community guidelines is removed. You can also hide any leaderboard on your own device. For anything urgent, email the address above.

Common questions

Why is an event or its results missing?

Results come from the organizers' timing services and appear once they publish them. Pull down to refresh, or check again later in the day.

I can't edit a time I posted, how do I fix this?

Posts are tied to the phone that made them. If you erased your phone or switched to a new one, email us and we will fix or remove the post for you.
