October 20, 2026 · Release

AutoX Live 2.0

  • Pull to refresh on every results and leaderboard page, even when it's empty or offline.
  • GGLC drivers show their car's make and model.
  • Share your Friends list: the Friends tab's menu copies you and your pins, fastest first, ready to paste into the group chat.
  • Bigger tap targets for buttons, tabs, and event rows on iPhone.
  • CarPlay shows your last run the same way it shows your friends.

October 18, 2026 · Outage

AutoX Live was down for several hours on 10/18 because Nutmeg, the server's resident cat, stepped on the power button. Everything is back up. Please direct all complaints to Nutmeg.

October 14, 2026 · Release

AutoX Live 1.5

  • Live results refresh themselves every 10 seconds on race day.
  • Notifications when a new time posts for you or a friend, even at an event you haven't opened yet.
  • Apple Watch: your last run and your friends' times on your wrist.
  • CarPlay: the same two pages on your car's screen.
  • No signal? The last results stay on your phone, with a note saying how old they are.
  • No TrackAddict log? Post a time with a photo as proof, and it goes up once it's reviewed.
  • AutoX Live for Android, read-only for now. Download it here.
