AutoX Live

Autocross timing & results for iPhone and Android

AutoX Live puts autocross timing in your pocket. See results as they post, keep an eye on your friends and rivals, and find out exactly where the time went.

Join the beta on TestFlight

Free. Needs an iPhone and Apple's TestFlight app.

Download for Android

Free. Android 8 or later. Your browser will ask you to allow installing apps from it the first time. The Android version is read-only for now: posting times and notifications are coming in a later version.

Live results

Open an event and see every car's best time, position, and run count. Switch between sessions, pull to refresh between runs, and tap any car for its run-by-run breakdown.

Friends

Star the cars you care about to get a short list of just your people, and mark your own car to see your gap to everyone else. Give cars nicknames, because "Silver BMW Coupe" is not a name.

Head-to-head

Compare any two drivers side by side: best, average, spread, and a chart of both drivers' times over the day.

Community leaderboards

Create a leaderboard for your course, test day, or club, or keep one unlisted and share its join code with just your group. Post a time by hand or import it from a TrackAddict log. Leaderboards are for times set at sanctioned events or on closed courses only.

No ads, no tracking

Browse without an account; Sign in with Apple only when you want to post. Your pins, nicknames, and settings stay on your phone.

Independent

AutoX Live is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any event organizer, club, or timing provider. Event results come from organizers' publicly available timing feeds and appear when the organizer publishes them.
